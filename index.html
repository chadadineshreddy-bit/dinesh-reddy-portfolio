5-Stage End-to-End Analytical Pipeline

The standard methodological framework Dinesh applies to transform ambiguous raw business datasets into dependable analytics.

01

Ingestion & Audit

Verify data integrity, check data types (strings, timestamps, numerics), and identify null value frequencies across initial tables via Pandas & SQL.

02

Cleaning & Imputation

Remediate skewed distributions with median imputation, eliminate corrupted duplicates, and apply IQR fencing to isolate extreme statistical outliers.

03

Feature Engineering

Derive meaningful domain features: customer tenure, RFM scores, seasonality indicators, moving averages, and normalized KPI ratios.

04

Exploratory & ML

Execute correlation matrices, hypothesis tests, and train supervised machine learning models (Random Forest, Regressors) to discover predictive patterns.

05

BI Dashboard Delivery

Design clean, uncluttered visual dashboards in Power BI or interactive web interfaces, translating complex statistical outputs into plain business actions.
